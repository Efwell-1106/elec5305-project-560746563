01 / THE STUDY

Does the requested change
appear in the measurements?

For one instrumental electronic-music prompt family, do Bright and Dark instructions produce consistent acoustic changes across matched seeds? Do other, unrequested attributes change too?

A

A fixed-signal DSP reference

Apply a symmetric FFT-domain spectral tilt to the same recording, from −1 to +1 in steps of 0.25. Recompute the descriptors after processing.

An engineering reference for measurable spectral change. No equal perceptual spacing is assumed.

B

A paired generated-music audit

Compare Neutral, Bright and Dark recordings for each of five shared seeds: 12315, 12325, 12335, 12345 and 12355. Measure target and other descriptors consistently.

ACE-Step audio; exact installed version unverified. Generation controls are based on the author's recollection.

02 / WHAT THE DATA SHOW

A repeatable DSP response.
A less consistent prompt response.

1/5

Bright raised centroid

Only one Bright recording had a higher centroid than its matched Neutral.

5/5

Dark lowered centroid

The direction was consistent, but one decrease was only about 0.52 Hz.

2/5

HF ratio followed direction

Each prompt condition matched its expected HF-ratio direction in two pairs.

Requested changes are only part of the question.

RMS, spectral flux and detected onset rate also varied. Their paired-change SDs exceeded the magnitudes of their mean changes. This sample does not establish selective control of brightness while preserving other attributes.

What this pilot cannot establish

One context and five seeds do not support a general claim about all generators. Complete generation logs and the exact model version were not retained. Exploratory pitch estimates accumulated near their upper search boundary and were excluded. No listening study, causal mechanism or statistical significance is claimed.

03 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

One App.
A shared analysis pipeline.

Import a recording, inspect it, apply the DSP reference and analyze the retained library. The App works with existing audio; it does not invoke a music-generation model.

01

Audio Workbench

Original and processed playback, waveform, FFT, STFT and acoustic descriptors.

02

Brightness Experiment

A nine-setting sweep with plots and a measurement table for the current recording.

03

Music Library

Batch measurements and descriptive condition summaries for all 15 recordings.

04

FM Synth & Methods

An optional test-tone generator and concise method notes. Separate from the prompt audit.

RUN LOCALLY

Download. Extract. Open in MATLAB.

Prepared in MATLAB R2026a Update 4. Core analysis requires no optional toolboxes; optional pitch uses Audio Toolbox.

run_project            % Launch the App
run_experiments        % Recompute audio measurements
run_summary_analysis   % Plot supplied paired measurements
